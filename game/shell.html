Kinesis

Best played in landscape

Move with the left stick (or WASD). Drag anywhere to aim.

Tap FORCE to gather stones — they ride a wheel behind you. Holding them is free.

The button then becomes SHOOT and stays there until the wheel is empty.

Single fires one stone per press, each bending into the crosshair. Burst throws the lot at once, unguided. MODE swaps them — 10s cooldown.

JUMP clears a swipe — walkers cannot reach you at the top of one. DASH blinks you out of a closing circle, 1.3s cooldown.

Not everything on the ground is a rock. Barrels detonate and set each other off, chem leaves a burning pool, boulders hit like a truck but haul slowly, planks and girders punch straight through a line of them.

Kill fast to build a combo. Chains and multi-kills score hardest.

Single is one heavy guided shot — head hits double it. Burst locks a different target per stone but each lands softer.

Runners are fast and frail. Armored and Tanks shrug off rocks — use boulders, girders or barrels. Exploders detonate when they die.

Keyboard — WASD move · Space jump · Shift dash · R push · F fire · Q mode
