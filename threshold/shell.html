Candidate assessment · in progress

You were told it was nine floors. You were told it was a formality — a short assessment, and then the real thing would begin.

You are on floor nine hundred and something. The Invigilator has not answered a question in a long time. The floors keep arriving.

The Threshold kills you. That is not a failure state, it is the format. Every death returns you to the first floor with nothing you were carrying.

What survives is the record. You keep the Marks the Threshold awards for depth reached, and you spend them on things it cannot take back.
