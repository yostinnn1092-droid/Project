Nebula

You are a constellation

You are a distant god. You have no hands in this world, no voice they can hear directly, no body to place upon the ground. You have only Faith — and the people who give it to you.

How it works. A people spreads across the world below. They farm, they build, they die, they grow — all without you. You cannot command them. You can only answer them.

  • Every settlement generates Faith — more when their Devotion is high.
  • They send you prayers. Answering costs Faith. Refusing costs Devotion.
  • If Devotion reaches zero, they abandon you. You end.

The trap. Answer every prayer and Devotion soars — but so does Dependency. A people who are always saved forget how to save themselves. They pray more. They produce less. They break at the first thing you cannot afford.

So you must refuse them. Choose which suffering makes them stronger, and which one ends them. That is the whole game.

Winning. Rival constellations are raising their own peoples. Grow yours to an empire of faith and reach Ascension before they do.
