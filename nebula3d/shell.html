You are a constellation

A god of the fourth magnitude

Below you is a world you cannot touch. There are people in it — actual people, each one walking somewhere for a reason of their own. You did not tell them to. You cannot tell them anything.

What you can do is answer.

Every person is real. Each has a name, a trade, a home, and a temperament. Each keeps their own private count of what you have done for them. Your Faith is the sum of what they individually believe — not a number the world hands you.

They decide for themselves. A frightened farmer stops farming. A hungry one goes looking for food instead of working. One who has stopped believing in you walks off the edge of the world and does not come back.

Temperament decides how they take an answer — and a refusal.

DevoutBelieves easily, forgives quickly. Slow to lose.
SkepticHard to convince, quick to write you off.
ZealotSwings hard both ways. Loudest, least stable.
StoicBarely moves. Neither miracle nor silence shifts them far.
PragmatistBelieves whatever the harvest says. Ignores wonders.
FrightenedFaith tracks safety. Danger nearby erodes it fast.

The trap is unchanged. Answer everything and Dependency climbs — they stop solving anything themselves, they pray for everything, and your income collapses under the weight of it. Refuse everything and they starve, or leave.

Choose whose suffering is worth it.
